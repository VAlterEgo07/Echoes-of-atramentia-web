Clause 1: Age Verification Architecture (Age Gate)

Upon launching the Videogame for the first time, the system will execute a mandatory, neutral Age Gate prompting the user to input their full date of birth. To uphold the principles of data minimization, this exact data is never transmitted to or cached on our remote servers. Instead, a local mathematical routine on the client-side device determines whether the player satisfies the minimum digital consent age required by their respective jurisdiction.

Clause 2: Restricted Privacy Mode for Minors

LEGAL GUARDIAN NOTICE: In the event that the client-side evaluation establishes that the user is below the minimum legal age of digital consent, the software will immediately enforce Restricted Privacy Mode. While Restricted Privacy Mode is active, the following automated limitations are strictly imposed:

  • Complete Telemetry Suppression: The transmission of all telemetry packets, including gameplay statistics, structural metrics related to the signature spell-drawing mechanic, and specific session durations, is completely deactivated. Only non-identifiable, localized technical crash dumps are gathered to ensure cross-platform software stability.
  • Communication Invalidation: All free-text, unstructured communication mechanisms, including text and voice chat implementations, are entirely disabled to protect minors from unauthorized exposure. Any collaborative or social functionality will be limited strictly to a deterministic system of predefined phrases and safe contextual pings.
  • Verifiable Parental Consent (VPC): Access to cloud-synchronized persistence, cross-device save states, or the downloading of community-curated cosmetic configurations requires the express, verifiable authorization of a parent or legal guardian. This verification process will be conducted via specialized third-party verification protocols in compliance with COPPA standards.

Clause 3: Data Lifecycle Framework (How Data is Captured, Used, Shared, and Retained)

For individuals who have legally surpassed the age of digital consent, Inkwell Studios processes only the minimal data footprint required to execute the digital contract and maintain game services. The data infrastructure operates strictly under the following lifecycle parameters:

  • 1. How Data is Captured (Collection): Data is captured automatically on the client-side device during the execution of the software. This includes technical and hardware identifiers (truncated IP addresses, system architecture specifications, GPU drivers, and memory allocations), as well as gameplay interface telemetry (vector coordinates of stroke trajectories drawn by the user, puzzle-solving durations, spell precision indexes, and contextual achievement states). Background automated monitoring tools also capture active system processes and volatile memory (RAM) exclusively while the game runs to detect unauthorized modifications.
  • 2. How Data is Used: Captured data is utilized solely for engine optimization, threat prevention, calibrating game difficulty, balancing spell recognition algorithms, preventing network synchronization errors, and anti-cheat enforcement to ensure a fair environment.
  • 3. How Data is Shared: Inkwell Studios does not sell, rent, or commercially share user data with third-party advertisers. Data transmission is strictly limited to necessary technical execution and specialized third-party verification protocols required to obtain Verifiable Parental Consent (VPC) under COPPA compliance standards.
  • 4. How Data is Retained: Data is retained only for the minimal duration required to maintain active game synchronization, player states, and legitimate software security parameters.

In accordance with the GDPR and CCPA, users (and verified guardians of minor users) retain comprehensive rights of Access, Rectification, Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), and Portability.
