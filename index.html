Characters & Combat System

1. The Art of Abilities

In the world of Atramentia, magic is not recited; it is drawn with patience and precision in your pocket diary:

  • Basic & Charged Attack: Usage of a spell pre-drawn in your notebook. No cooldown. Charged attacks require maintaining the stroke for longer duration or power.
  • Quick Spell: Activated with cooldowns. They require drawing the magic pattern at high speed; the quality and fidelity of the stroke define the damage dealt.
  • Personal Spell: Each character's ultimate and complex technique. Unleashes devastating power under unique combat conditions.
  • Combat Passive: Ink spirits that actively assist (summons) or imbue mystical enhancements (buffs) permanently or after combos.
  • Exploration Passive: Daily talents that improve camp life, such as cooking, plant gathering, and cartography.

3. Elemental Essences

Ink imbues natural properties to the paper. Elements manifest according to the flow of mystical energy used:

Fire Wind Water Earth Light Darkness Aether

Geometry of Magic Circles

The placement of ink alters the final result of the spell:

  • In the Center: Defines the primordial element and spell core.
  • On the Margins: Acts as a secondary catalyst or reaction trigger.
  • Overlapping Circles: Intertwining multiple elements and modifiers creates complex spells.
