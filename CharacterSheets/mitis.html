Mitis

⭐⭐⭐⭐
Role: Destruction
Elements:

Bond & History

You meet at the city gates after being assigned to the same mission. You quickly become friends, discovering that Mitis joined the guild out of a genuine desire to help others.

After completing the mission, you share your reasons for joining the guild, and Mitis decides to join your party, becoming a definitive member of your team.

Personality

A young man who loves helping others, always wearing a constant smile. He is tireless, endlessly searching for adventures and joy. However, when faced with injustice, his demeanor shifts, and he does not hesitate to act with firm resolve.

Elemental Domain & Spells

  • Light (DPS): Magic attacks focusing on direct damage and dazzling enemies through photon concentration in ink.
  • Fire (DPS): Terra passione: Creates a small fire field on the ground that inflicts constant damage to enemies within the area.
  • Wind (DPS): Lamina ventus / Cultro ventus: Launches gusts of wind blades and a giant air knife with great impact power.
